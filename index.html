OUR STORY

We started in a small garage in 2019 with a single workbench, a few hand tools, and an obsession with things that last.

No algorithms. No assembly lines. Every piece passes through human hands from raw timber to finished object. We work with local mills, choose materials that age well, and refuse to rush.

The marks you see — small tool lines, grain variation, the slight unevenness in a joint — these aren't flaws. They're proof.

— Marcus & Elena
Founders, The Workshop Co.

THE PROCESS

01
🪵

SOURCE

We visit local mills. Touch the timber. Choose by feel and grain. No compromises on material.

→
02
📐

SKETCH

Paper and pencil. Every design begins by hand. No CAD, no renderings — just honest drawings.

→
03
🔨

BUILD

Chisels, planes, saws. Traditional joinery. Each piece is fitted, not fastened. No nails in our furniture.

→
04

Hand-sanded through 5 grits. Oiled and waxed. Signed. Shipped with care in recycled packaging.
